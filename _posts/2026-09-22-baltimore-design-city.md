A couple of weeks ago I went down to Baltimore for the opening of Reference, a design collective curated by Sam Acuff, who cofounded the craft-oriented furniture maker Koba. I went for a day. I wish I'd stayed the week.

One train ride south of New York, there's a city quietly using design to make joy, community, and the kind of togetherness the rest of us keep writing manifestos about. I found an extraordinary group of makers doing serious work in buildings with good bones and cheap rent. If you read the history of any design scene anywhere, that's exactly the recipe.

Here's what I saw.

## Reference

A living archive. An appointment-only studio where objects, art, and material samples are shown with reverence and purpose. It's less a showroom than a shared reference point. The idea is simple: a place where great work can live, be seen, and start a conversation without the usual noise, built around the people and practices they admire. Cohort 01 is the small first group setting the tone for what Reference becomes.

It's housed in a 1922 library, and it is beautifully appointed. I have stood in a lot of libraries. This is the first one I didn't want to leave.

## Good Neighbor

A design shop and café in an old Hampden hardware store, with a guesthouse upstairs. Shawn Chopra and Anne Morgan opened it in 2020, and it has become the neighborhood's living room. Morgan: "Even if you can't buy the <span class="nb">fifty-dollar</span> mug, you can still drink from it while you're here." They were running a Baltimore design week back in 2021.

## The makers

*Koba* — Sam's furniture line, named after a punk izakaya in Hiroshima and built out of the Good Neighbor Design Garage. It has nine years of fabrication behind it: playful, a little lo-fi, made to last. Look for the Milk Crate.

* * *

Eindhoven became Eindhoven because people stayed and built things, then gave themselves a week to show them off. Baltimore already has the pieces: a shop that throws its own design week, a fabricator turned furniture brand, and now a library full of references.

An American Eindhoven. <span aria-hidden="true">🪑</span>
